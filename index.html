Dresser Lights

Controls Bluetooth LED strips that normally use the Lotus Lantern app (ELK-BLEDOM type).

This browser can't talk to Bluetooth devices. Open this page in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge (on a Mac, PC or Android phone). Safari and iPhone browsers don't support it — on iPhone, the free Bluefy browser does.
